# OSK interface system

## Colour

Storefront: near-black, OSK orange and white with warm neutral catalogue sections. Admin: dark green-black sidebar #141a17, neutral canvas #f5f5f2, white work surfaces, orange #e95e22 for primary actions. Green badges mean confirmed or in stock; amber means pending or needs attention. Do not use colour as the only status cue.

## Typography

DM Sans for operations, tables, labels and forms. Barlow Condensed for storefront headlines. Admin headings stay restrained; operational screens prioritise readable data over brand headlines. Prices and quantities use tabular numerals.

## Layout

The storefront lives at /. Admin lives at /admin, with a persistent workspace sidebar. Tables support search, filters and 20-row display pages. Edit one record in a modal side panel, keeping list context. On narrow screens, the sidebar becomes an icon rail with accessible names; tables scroll inside their panel.

## Components

Use consistent panels, compact status badges, bordered secondary actions and one orange primary action per form. Native dialog panels provide modal keyboard focus and Escape-to-close. Form errors remain visible inside the modal. Show actual storage and connection state; demo content must be labelled. Stock edits go through a reasoned adjustment form with a movement history. Preserve archived records and order snapshots.
